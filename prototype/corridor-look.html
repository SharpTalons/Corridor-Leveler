What changed

  • Charcoal graph with a soft top-to-bottom gradient and faint grid, like the reference. Input level is the grey line, output is the bold red line.
  • Matte black dials with a thin yellow value ring and a yellow dot pointer. Threshold's ring runs green to yellow; its line in the graph is green.
  • Threshold and Response on the left, a larger Target in the middle with the Ride / Hold switch under it, Range and Ceiling on the right.
  • Slim green meters with an olive unlit track, yellow above -6 dBFS and red above -1, yellow peak ticks, numbers on top.
  • Grey raised buttons; an active option lights its text yellow. The controls sit on the graph and fade it out, with no panel box.
  • Applied gain is in the readout box at the bottom right, and latency at the bottom left.
