What changes

  • Every control is glass: it blurs and slightly magnifies the graph behind it, with a bright rim where the light catches the edge. The rim follows your pointer.
  • The header and the two meters float over a full-bleed graph. The controls have no box: the graph frosts and fades into the window colour under them, so they sit in the graph itself.
  • Dials are clear glass discs with a light value ring and pointer. Ride / Hold is a glass capsule whose selected side is a droplet that slides across.
  • Each control has its own colour, used on its dial ring, pointer, value and line in the graph: Target teal (and the output it aims at), Threshold amber, Response blue, Range pink, Ceiling coral. The window, glass and text stay neutral. Dark mode uses softer versions; light mode uses deeper ones so their words stay readable.
  • Meters use the standard zones fixed to the scale: green up to −18 dBFS, amber to −6, red above, with a clip light on top and the peak readout in its zone colour. Their ticks and numbers sit at exactly the graph's heights, every 5 dB with a number every 10.
  • Response is a time, from 10 ms (very fast, follows every word) to 3000 ms (slow, rides whole phrases), on a log scale so the fast end has room.
  • Applied gain reads blue while lifting and orange while lowering, with its sign; the status dot matches. Colour is never the only signal: every state also has words.
  • Dark by default; the sun / moon switch turns the plugin light. Hold slides a glass sheet out under the window with the live waveform.
