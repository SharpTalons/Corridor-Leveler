Corridor Leveler

Interactive prototype · Windows VST3 plugin for OBS and DAWs

Set a target and Corridor keeps your audio there. Ride mode moves the gain continuously. Hold mode analyzes a section, then locks one fixed correction. The window below runs live on test audio, and you can also play your own file.

How it works

Two modes

Ride moves the gain all the time, like a hand riding a fader. Use it for voices and anything whose level keeps changing. Hold measures a typical section when you press Analyze, works out one correction, and locks it. Use it for steady material.

Measuring

The level it corrects is measured as RMS, the average power over 300 ms. The meters on both edges show peak level in dBFS: input on the left, output on the right.

Five controls

  • Target: the RMS level you want.
  • Threshold: below this it stops adjusting.
  • Response: slow and smooth, or fast and responsive.
  • Range: the most it may boost or cut.
  • Ceiling: the highest peak the output may reach. A look-ahead brick-wall limiter catches stray peaks.
